# Indulgences for the Plano de Vida Practices

*A research report on the indulgences attached to each spiritual practice in the app, per the Catholic Church's official* Enchiridion Indulgentiarum *(Manual of Indulgences, 4th ed., 1999), the Code of Canon Law, and Paul VI's* Indulgentiarum Doctrina *(1967).*

---

## 1. What an indulgence is

An **indulgence** remits the *temporal punishment* due to sins that have already been forgiven. Two kinds:

- **Partial** — removes *part* of that punishment. No fixed "days" anymore (that system was abolished in 1967); the amount mirrors the fervor of the act.
- **Plenary** — removes *all* of it.

---

## 2. Indulgences per practice

| Practice (PT) | Indulgence | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| **Rosário** | **Plenary** | Five decades recited **continuously**, with **vocal recitation + meditation on the mysteries**, said **in a church/oratory, family, religious community, or pious association**. **Partial** if said alone/privately. |
| **Visita ao Santíssimo** | **Plenary** | Eucharistic adoration of **at least half an hour**. **Partial** for a shorter visit. |
| **Leitura do Evangelho / Leitura Espiritual** (Sacred Scripture) | **Plenary** | Reading Scripture as spiritual reading, from an approved text, with reverence, for **at least half an hour**. **Partial** for any shorter reading. Applies to **Scripture**; spiritual reading of *other* books is not separately indulgenced. |
| **Meditação** (mental prayer) | **Partial** | "To those who spend some time in pious mental prayer." |
| **Angelus** (and Regina Caeli in Eastertide) | **Partial** | Reciting the Angelus / Regina Caeli. |
| **Lembrai-vos** (the Memorare) | **Partial** | Recitation of the Memorare. |
| **Comunhão Espiritual** | **Partial** | An act of spiritual communion using any pious formula. |
| **Novena a São Josemaria / Novena do Trabalho** | **Partial** (conditional) | The Enchiridion grants a partial indulgence for a **public novena before Christmas, Pentecost, or the Immaculate Conception**. A novena to St. Josemaría doesn't fall under that specific grant, but its prayers earn partial indulgence under the **general grants** (pious invocation / offering of work). |
| **Oferecimento de Obras / Oferecimento do Trabalho** (morning offering / offering of work) | **Partial** | Not a named item, but covered by **General Grant 1** (raising the mind to God while doing one's duties). |
| **Oração a São Josemaria** | **Partial** (general) | No specific concession in the Enchiridion. Earns partial indulgence as a pious invocation (General Grant 1). |
| **Preces da Obra** | — | Opus Dei community prayer; not in the universal Enchiridion. The Prelature may hold particular concessions (unverified) — treat as not universally indulgenced. |
| **Exame de Consciência** | — (disposition) | Not a separately indulgenced act. It's a recommended disposition and supports the "detachment from sin" condition for plenary indulgences. (A *plenary* attaches to the year-end **Te Deum** and New-Year **Veni Creator**, not to the daily exam.) |
| **Três Ave-Marias** | — | Indulgenced in the **old Raccolta**, but **not carried into** the current 1999 Manual. No specific grant today (still earns partial as devout prayer). |
| **Missa** (attending Holy Mass) | — / Plenary (special cases) | Attending Mass is not itself listed as an indulgenced "work." But Communion received at Mass fulfills a plenary condition, and **plenary** indulgences attach to special occasions (a priest's first Mass, your First Communion, 25th/50th/60th ordination anniversaries, etc.). |

### Bottom line for the plan of life

- **Can yield a PLENARY indulgence (with the 4 conditions below):** Rosário (in community), Visita ao Santíssimo (≥30 min), Leitura do Evangelho/Espiritual *if Sacred Scripture* (≥30 min).
- **Yield a PARTIAL indulgence:** Meditação, Angelus, Lembrai-vos, Comunhão Espiritual, Rosário (said alone), Scripture reading under 30 min, the morning/work offerings, prayer to St. Josemaría, the novena, and Três Ave-Marias.
- **No specific grant:** the daily Exame de Consciência and Preces da Obra (though the exam strengthens the dispositions a plenary requires).

## 3. The four "general grants" (each a PARTIAL indulgence)

These sweep up many of the items above. A partial indulgence is granted to the faithful who:

1. **Raise their minds in humble trust to God** while carrying out duties and bearing life's hardships, adding (at least mentally) a pious invocation. → *covers morning offering, offering of work, ejaculatory prayers, prayer to St. Josemaría*
2. **Give of themselves or their goods** in a spirit of faith and mercy to serve those in need.
3. **Voluntarily abstain, in a spirit of penance**, from something licit and pleasing to them.
4. **Openly bear witness to their faith** before others in the circumstances of daily life.

---

## 4. To whom can the indulgence be applied?

Both yourself or a deceased soul — **your choice each time** (Code of Canon Law, can. 994):

- **For yourself** — always an option.
- **For the dead** (souls in purgatory) — applied *per modum suffragii* ("by way of suffrage"): you offer it as intercession, and God applies it as He sees fit, so it is not a guaranteed transfer the way self-application is.
- **You cannot apply an indulgence to another living person.**

Practical points:

- It's an **either/or per act**: each indulgenced work goes to *either* yourself *or* one deceased soul. You can't split one indulgence between both.
- You don't need to name a specific soul — you can offer it generally "for the souls in purgatory."
- The one-plenary-per-day limit applies regardless of who you offer it for.

## 5. Conditions to actually earn an indulgence

### Prerequisites for *any* indulgence (plenary or partial)

You must be:

- **Baptized** and **not excommunicated**
- **In the state of grace** — at least by the time you finish the indulgenced work
- Acting with **at least a general intention** to gain the indulgence (a habitual intention, e.g. "I intend to gain all the indulgences I can," renewed from time to time, is enough)
- Performing the prescribed work **at the time and in the manner required**

### Partial indulgence — that's essentially it

Only the prerequisites above plus doing the indulgenced act. **No** confession, Communion, or papal prayer required. You can gain **multiple partial indulgences in a single day**. The Church supplements the remission in proportion to the charity and contrition you bring.

### Plenary indulgence — the prerequisites *plus* four more things

1. **The indulgenced work itself** (e.g. Rosary in community, ½-hour adoration, ½-hour Scripture reading).
2. **Sacramental Confession**
3. **Eucharistic Communion**
4. **Prayer for the intentions of the Supreme Pontiff** — minimum one *Our Father* + one *Hail Mary* (you may add more). This means the Pope's general intentions for the Church, **not** his private intentions.
5. **Complete detachment from all sin, even venial.** The hardest condition. If any attachment to sin remains — or any of the above is missing — the indulgence is **reduced to partial**.

### Timing & frequency rules for plenary

- **Same-day not strictly required:** Confession, Communion, and the papal prayer may be done **several days before or after** the work — official guidance puts this at **about 20 days** before or after. It is *fitting* that Communion and the papal prayer happen on the day of the work.
- **One Confession → many plenaries:** a single sacramental confession can serve for **several** plenary indulgences over multiple days.
- **One Communion + one papal prayer → one plenary:** these two must be **repeated separately for each** plenary indulgence.
- **One plenary per day maximum** — exception: a person *in danger of death* (*in articulo mortis*) can receive one even if already gained one that day, and even without a priest if they habitually prayed during life (using a crucifix is recommended).

### Bonus rule

If an indulgenced work is **also** assigned as a sacramental penance in Confession, the **same act** both satisfies the penance *and* earns the indulgence.

---

## 6. Practical takeaway

- The **partial-indulgence** practices (Meditação, Angelus, Lembrai-vos, Comunhão Espiritual, offerings, etc.) just need you in a state of grace with the intention — do them freely all day; they stack.
- For a **plenary** (Rosary in family, ½-hr visit, ½-hr Scripture), line up regular Confession + daily Communion + the papal prayer, and — the real work — strive for detachment from all sin.
- You can secure **one plenary per day**; everything else accumulates as partials, applicable to yourself or a soul in purgatory.
